📱 Reported Speech Trainer

Quick Reminder

In reported speech, we often move one step back:

  • am/is → was
  • are → were
  • have/has → had
  • have had → had had
  • will → would
  • can → could
  • this → that
  • here → there
  • now → then
Real Life vs Textbook
Textbook: She said she had just had her interview.
Conversation: She said she just had her interview.
For this exercise, choose the textbook version.
